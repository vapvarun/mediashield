# The admin menu has six groups and loses no screen

## Check lines

1. card:10530930846 - As an administrator open MediaShield. **Expect** six top items in the app menu: Dashboard, Videos, Analytics, Security, Platforms, Settings. Open each of `#/dashboard #/videos #/playlists #/tags #/upload-queue #/viewers #/students #/heatmap #/realtime #/milestones #/export #/alerts #/platforms #/bunny-browser #/settings #/license #/milestone-config`: **Expect** each opens its own screen (not the Dashboard fallback), its group is highlighted and the screen is marked current under it. With Pro inactive: **Expect** still six items, Security and Platforms shown locked with PRO, and Heatmaps, Realtime and Export locked under Analytics. At 390px: **Expect** the six icons on one row and the open group's screens on a second row, each a 40px target. **On fail**: `src/admin/components/Sidebar.js` (`GROUPS`), `src/admin/admin.css` (`.mediashield-sidebar__group`, `.mediashield-sidebar__sub`).
2. card:10530930901 - Give one viewer four sessions today on one video and its 25/50/75/100 milestones; open Dashboard, Last 7 days. **Expect** the Activity chart shows the week with a visible point today; Top Videos reads "1 view" / "N views"; Recent Milestones has one line for that viewer and video reading "Name - finished"; a Protection card shows "Videos protected X of Y" (and with Pro, Blocked attempts and Alerts as numbers, without Pro "Recorded with Pro"). On a fresh install (no published video, no sessions): **Expect** a "Get started" checklist with three steps (Add a video, Protect it, Place it on a page), each ticked as it is done and gone when all three are. 1440px and 390px. **On fail**: `includes/REST/AnalyticsController.php` (`fill_days()`, `recent_milestones()`, `protected_counts()`, `first_run()`), `src/admin/pages/Dashboard.js`.
3. card:10530930776 - Open Settings at 1440px. **Expect** a section list on the left (Protection, Watermark, Player, Access & messages, Video ads, Upload & storage, Integrations when an LMS is active) and a search box. Click Player: **Expect** the Player Controls card scrolls to the top. Type "IP": **Expect** only cards mentioning it stay (Watermark, Pro Watermark), with "N sections match."; type "zzz": **Expect** "No settings match."; clear it: **Expect** every card back. Toggle any setting: **Expect** "Saving…" then "Saved". With Pro: **Expect** a "Watermark fields" entry after Watermark that jumps to the Pro Watermark card, which sits inside the settings column with nothing cut off at 1440, 1024 and 390. Scroll to the bottom at 1440 and 1024: **Expect** the section list still in view (top 46px). At 390px: **Expect** the list is a "Go to section" select that jumps to the section. **On fail**: `src/admin/pages/Settings.js` (`SettingsNav`, `SETTINGS_GROUPS`), `src/admin/admin.css` (`.mediashield-settings__layout`).
4. card:10533275569 (Learnomy link to Lesson Completion) - With Learnomy active open `admin.php?page=mediashield#/settings/integrations` in a new tab at 1440px and 390px. **Expect** Settings (not the Dashboard) with Integrations marked in the section list (the select at 390) and the Lesson Completion card in view, focused. With Pro open `#/settings/pro-watermark`: **Expect** the Pro Watermark card at the top. On Settings change the hash to `#/settings/player`: **Expect** the Player card scrolls to the top without a reload. Open `#/settings/nope`: **Expect** Settings at the top with the address reset to `#/settings`. Click Video ads in the section list: **Expect** the address reads `#/settings/ads`. `#/videos` and `#/dashboard` still open their own screens. **On fail**: `src/admin/App.js` (sub-path route match), `src/admin/pages/Settings.js` (`linkedGroup()`, the deep-link effect in `SettingsNav`).
