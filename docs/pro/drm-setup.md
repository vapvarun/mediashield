# Encryption and DRM

MediaShield Pro does not encrypt or package video, and it has no DRM screen. Since 1.4.1 the DRM protection level, the DRM page and the licence route are removed.

MediaShield controls who can start playback: login, roles, stream limits, allowed domains and the watermark. If you need encryption or DRM, use your video host's own, such as Bunny Stream's.

See [Protection Philosophy](../free/protection-philosophy.md) for what each layer can and cannot stop.
