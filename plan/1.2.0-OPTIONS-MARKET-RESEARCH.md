# MediaShield 1.2.0 — Options Market Research & Prioritized Roadmap

> **Purpose.** Compile the complete set of controls/options that buyers in the video-protection + player category expect, so MediaShield can expose them as settings (global + per-video). Each control is tagged **table-stakes** (everyone ships it; absence is a deal-breaker) vs **premium differentiator** (a paid-tier or category-leader feature). The report closes with a prioritized **OPTIONS ROADMAP** tagging every control `[HAS]` / `[PARTIAL]` / `[GAP]` against MediaShield's current build.
>
> **Scope guardrail.** MediaShield is a *player for gated, member-only course/lesson video* (see `plan/PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`). Public-distribution features (share buttons, public view counts, trending feeds) are out of scope by contract and are **excluded** from the roadmap regardless of how common they are in OTT competitors.
>

## 2. Protection / anti-piracy

| Control | What it does | Who offers it | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dynamic watermark** | Burns viewer email/IP/ID onto video, ideally moving position + opacity. | VdoCipher (moving, configurable opacity/size/color), Bunny, Spotlightr | Table-stakes for this category |
| **Widevine DRM** | Browser DRM (Chrome/Firefox/Edge). | VdoCipher, Bunny, Vimeo | Table-stakes (premium tier) |
| **FairPlay DRM** | Apple DRM (Safari/iOS/macOS) — required to actually block capture on Apple. | VdoCipher, Bunny | **Differentiator** (gap vs leaders) |
| **PlayReady DRM** | MS DRM (smart TVs / Edge legacy). | VdoCipher | Niche differentiator |
| **Domain / referrer whitelist** | Embed plays only on allowed domains. | Spotlightr, VdoCipher, Bunny hotlink protection | Table-stakes |
| **Right-click / devtools block** | Deters casual source-grabbing. | Most WP plugins, Spotlightr | Table-stakes (weak protection, expected anyway) |
| **Screen-capture deterrence** | DRM/HDCP blocks capture on apps + Safari; watermark deters elsewhere. | VdoCipher (apps + Safari via Widevine/FairPlay) | **Differentiator** — only real via FairPlay/HDCP |
| **Signed / expiring / tokenized URLs** | Time-limited playback token (OTP). | VdoCipher OTP, Bunny token auth | Table-stakes (premium) |
| **Download prevention** | No download via tools/plugins. | VdoCipher, Bunny, all DRM hosts | Table-stakes |
| **Concurrent-stream limit** | Max simultaneous sessions/devices per user. | VdoCipher, Uscreen, Vimeo | Table-stakes (premium) |
| **IP restriction** | Allow/deny by IP. | VdoCipher, Uscreen | Differentiator |
| **Geo-blocking** | Allow/deny by country. | VdoCipher, Spotlightr, Vimeo, Uscreen | **Table-stakes for OTT; GAP for MediaShield** |

> **Reality check on screen-capture:** right-click/devtools blocking is theatre against a determined pirate. Only **HDCP enforced via Widevine L1 / FairPlay** actually black-screens a hardware capture, and only on supported app/Safari contexts. Everywhere else, the **moving dynamic watermark** is the real deterrent (it makes a leak traceable). MediaShield already leans on the watermark — correct strategy.

## 5. Compliance / e-learning  *(the category MediaShield is being pulled toward — BeyondTheCLE)*

> **Critical distinction that runs through this whole section:** there are two very different things often both called "no-skip":
> 1. **Gating the "Mark Complete" button** — cosmetic. The learner can still scrub to the final seconds and satisfy the gate. This is what **LearnDash native Video Progression** does — it is explicitly described as cheatable ([Spotlightr LearnDash guide](https://spotlightr.com/complete-guide-learndash-video-progression-feature/)).
> 2. **Actually disabling forward-seek / the scrubber** — real enforcement. Requires a player-layer control. Native to **Tutor LMS Pro** ("Restrict forward seeking", [v2.2.4](https://tutorlms.com/blog/tutor-lms-update-v2-2-4/)) and **LifterLMS Advanced Videos** ("Disable Controls", [advanced-videos](https://lifterlms.com/product/advanced-videos/)); add-on/player-layer for LearnDash (Spotlightr "Disable Forward Seek").
>
> **MediaShield is positioned to own #2 as a player layer** — and to go further into the accreditation cluster that no WordPress plugin ships.

### LMS native capabilities (the baseline MediaShield rides on top of)

| LMS | Force full watch | True forward-seek disable | Auto-complete on end | Idle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LearnDash** (free core) | Video Progression (gates Mark Complete; can hide play bar) | **No** — needs a player layer | Yes (+ completion delay) | "Video Pause on Window Unfocused" toggle only ([liquidweb](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/learndash/course-management/video-progression/)) |
| **Tutor LMS Pro** | Yes (% completion, Strict mode) | **Yes** — "Restrict forward seeking" | Yes | No |
| **LifterLMS** (Advanced Videos add-on) | "Require Video Completion" | **Yes** — "Disable Controls" removes scrubber | Auto-Play/Advance | No |

None of the three ship periodic attention prompts, in-video codes, affidavits, or watch-record-bound certificates — the accreditation-critical cluster.

### Accreditation requirements (CLE/CME self-study / on-demand)

| Control | What it does | Who offers it | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Force full watch / no-skip (true seek disable)** | Learner cannot fast-forward; full watch before completion. CME enduring-material players literally show "fast forwarding has been disabled… after the video is completed, a button to continue appears" ([Emory](https://med.emory.edu/education/cme/enduring-materials-guide.html)). BARBRI disables the scrubber via PlayPosit; Lawline rewinds on a missed checkpoint. | Tutor Pro, Lifter, Spotlightr, BARBRI/Lawline | **Strong norm. Note:** some platforms (CE21, PLI) *allow* free navigation and rely on attention checks instead — so the *verification* below is what regulators actually mandate. **GAP for MediaShield** |
| **Periodic attention verification** | Timed "are you still there?" prompts the learner must acknowledge to prove ongoing presence. | CE21, Lawline, PLI, West, BARBRI | **Table-stakes for accredited CLE; GAP — highest-value for BeyondTheCLE** |
| **In-video transcribed verification codes** | Alphanumeric codes shown/spoken at random times, recorded by learner, submitted on affirmation. **NY spec:** ≥1 code per ~50 min, never at start or end; **provider must independently verify codes and may NOT rely solely on log-in/log-out times** ([NY Courts](https://ww2.nycourts.gov/attorneys/cle/attendanceverification_info.shtml)). | **CE21** (configurable participation codes) is the standout; required in NY | **Differentiator (required in stricter states); GAP** |
| **Idle / inactivity detection** | Pause / require re-engagement when tab unfocused or no interaction; don't accrue credit-time while idle. Lawline uses a 3-min beeping countdown; West prompts "Are you still there?" | Lawline, West; WP: LearnDash unfocus toggle only | **Required in effect; near-absent in WP tooling; GAP** (MediaShield's "inactive session" cron is server cleanup, *not* viewer attention) |
| **Attendance affidavit / attestation** | Post-program affirmation the learner submits (often where codes are entered) to claim credit. | Every accredited CLE provider (BARBRI Attendance Affirmation, NY attorney affirmation) | **Table-stakes for CLE; GAP** |
| **Watch-time verification / certified audit log** | Append-only certified record of seconds watched, retained for audit. ACCME requires providers to "determine who participated… to appropriately award credit" ([ACCME](https://www.accme.org/faq/how-enduring-material-activity-defined)); NY says log-in/log-out alone is insufficient. In WP today only **GrassBlade xAPI + an LRS** does per-second logging. | CE21, Freestone, Lawline; WP: GrassBlade+LRS | **Table-stakes for accredited use; GAP** |
| **Certificate bound to a verifiable watch record** | Certificate issuance gated on verified attendance (codes + watch time), not mere page completion. | All commercial CLE platforms | **Table-stakes; GAP** (usually delegated to LMS — but the *watch record* it must bind to is MediaShield's to provide) |
| **Auto-complete on full watch** | Mark lesson complete when video ends. | LearnDash, Tutor, Lifter | Table-stakes for LMS — MediaShield **HAS** (100% milestone → auto-complete) |

> **WordPress white-space (the product thesis):** no WordPress plugin ships the accreditation-critical cluster — **periodic attention prompts + in-video transcribed codes + affidavit + watch-record-bound certificate**. **CE21** is the only commercial product bundling all four as configurable modes; the closest WP plugin, **Uncanny CE Credits for LearnDash** ([uncannyowl](https://www.uncannyowl.com/continuing-education-credits-for-learndash/)), only does credit tracking + completion-based certificates, not the verification cluster. A defensible MediaShield design supports **both** pop-up checkpoints **and** NY-style transcribed codes (≥1/50 min, never at start/end), captured on an affirmation, with provider-side verification gating the certificate.

## PRIORITIZED OPTIONS ROADMAP

Legend: **[HAS]** shipped · **[PARTIAL]** partially present / needs a real setting · **[GAP]** not built.
MediaShield exposes these as **global defaults + per-video overrides**.

### Tier 0 — Headline gap the client asked for
- `[GAP]` **Forward-seek / no-skip prevention** — clamp seek to furthest-watched point on `timeupdate`; rewind allowed, fast-forward blocked. Implement in `assets/js/player-wrapper.js` (the adapters already expose `seekTo`/`getPosition`; add a `maxWatched` ceiling). Global toggle + per-video override + "block scrubbing entirely" sub-option (hide timeline, Spotlightr-style). **This is the BeyondTheCLE blocker.**

### Tier 1 — Highest-value CLE/CE compliance gaps (directly monetizable for BeyondTheCLE; this cluster is the WordPress white-space no plugin ships)
- `[GAP]` **Periodic attention-verification prompts** — timed "are you still there?" checkpoints the learner must click to continue; fail → pause + don't accrue watch-time. **The single highest-value compliance gap** (every accredited CLE platform has it).
- `[GAP]` **In-video transcribed verification codes** — distinct from prompts: configurable codes surfaced at random times that the learner records and submits on the affirmation. Design to the **NY spec** (≥1 code per ~50 min, never at start/end) and provide **provider-side verification** (login/logout alone is non-compliant). CE21 is the template.
- `[GAP]` **Idle / inactivity detection** (Page Visibility API + no-interaction timer; pause + stop accruing credit-time; precedent: Lawline 3-min beep, West "are you still there"). *Note: existing "inactive session" cron is server cleanup, not viewer attention — do not conflate.*
- `[GAP]` **Watch-time verification / certified audit log** — append-only per-user record of seconds actually watched (builds on the existing `ms_watch_sessions` / milestone tables) for credit + audit; the record the certificate binds to.
- `[GAP]` **Attendance affidavit / attestation** — post-completion affirmation gate (also the surface where transcribed codes are submitted) before certificate/credit.
- `[GAP]` **Certificate gated on the watch record** — issue only when verified attendance (codes + watch-time) passes, not on page completion. Cert rendering can delegate to the LMS, but the *pass/fail signal* is MediaShield's to provide.
- `[PARTIAL→HAS]` **Auto-complete on full watch** — already have 25/50/75/100% milestones + LMS auto-complete; expose explicitly as a "require 100% before complete" compliance toggle.

### Tier 2 — Table-stakes player features customers expect on day one
- `[GAP]` **Captions / subtitles** (VTT upload, multi-language, styling) — also an ADA/accessibility requirement, not optional for compliance buyers.
- `[GAP]` **Chapters / markers** — clickable timeline jump points (note: pairs with no-skip — chapters should not let learners jump *ahead*).
- `[GAP]` **Picture-in-Picture** toggle (cheap; native API).
- `[PARTIAL]` **Playback-rate cap** — speed control exists; add an admin **max-speed cap** (e.g. ≤1.25x) for compliance video.
- `[GAP]` **Loop**, `[GAP]` **autoplay-muted** option (low priority, cheap).

### Tier 3 — Access & protection gaps that move MediaShield toward the OTT-grade set
- `[GAP]` **Geo-blocking** (allow/deny by country) — table-stakes across VdoCipher/Spotlightr/Vimeo/Uscreen; currently absent.
- `[GAP]` **IP restriction** (allow/deny lists) — pairs naturally with the existing concurrent-stream/session layer.
- `[GAP]` **Time-limited / scheduled access** (available-from / expires-on / N-days-after-enrollment).
- `[GAP]` **FairPlay DRM** (Pro) — Widevine is shipped; FairPlay is the missing half for real Apple/Safari + HDCP screen-capture protection.
- `[GAP]` **Password protection** (per-video) — simplest gate, currently relies on login/role only.

### Tier 4 — Pipeline / deliberate near-term
- `[GAP]` **Pre/mid/post-roll + overlay ads** on lesson video — already the next BeyondTheCLE pipeline item (Bunny + MediaShield + wb-ad-manager). Build as planned, not as an ad-hoc gap.

### Already strong — keep / surface as explicit settings  `[HAS]`
- `[HAS]` Dynamic watermark (email/IP) · `[HAS]` Login gate · `[HAS]` Role-based access · `[HAS]` Allowed-domain whitelist · `[HAS]` Right-click + devtools block · `[HAS]` Concurrent-stream limit · `[HAS]` Milestones 25/50/75/100% + LMS auto-complete · `[HAS]` Speed · `[HAS]` Sticky player · `[HAS]` Keyboard shortcuts · `[HAS]` Resume · `[HAS]` End-screen CTA · `[HAS]` Widevine DRM (Pro) · `[HAS]` Multi-platform (Bunny/YouTube/Vimeo/Wistia/self) · `[HAS]` Signed stream handoff (self-hosted) · `[HAS]` Email gate (Pro) · `[HAS]` Per-user/heatmap/realtime/suspicious-activity analytics (Pro).

## Top 10 highest-value gaps (ranked)

1. **Forward-seek / no-skip prevention (true seek-disable)** — the client's explicit ask and the gate to the entire CLE/CE market. Note this means *disabling the scrubber*, not just gating Mark Complete (which LearnDash already does and is cheatable).
2. **Periodic attention-verification prompts** ("are you still there?") — accreditors expect engagement verification for on-demand credit; every CLE platform has it, no WP plugin does.
3. **Watch-time verification / certified audit log** — the per-second record accreditors require to award credit; the thing the certificate must bind to. Only GrassBlade+LRS does this in WP today.
4. **In-video transcribed verification codes** — required in stricter states (NY: ≥1/50 min, never start/end, provider-verified); CE21 is the only commercial template.
5. **Idle / inactivity detection** — credit-time must not accrue while the learner is away; near-absent in WP tooling.
6. **Attendance affidavit / attestation gate** — standard CLE step before certificate; also the code-submission surface.
7. **Captions / subtitles (VTT, multi-lang)** — table-stakes player feature + ADA accessibility requirement.
8. **Geo-blocking (by country)** — table-stakes across every OTT competitor; currently absent.
9. **Chapters / markers** — expected course-player navigation (constrained to not bypass no-skip).
10. **FairPlay DRM (Pro)** — completes the DRM story for Apple/Safari + the only real screen-capture block via HDCP.

*(Honorable mentions just outside the 10: certificate-bound-to-watch-record, time-limited/scheduled access, playback-rate cap, IP restriction, per-video password, Picture-in-Picture.)*

> **Strategic takeaway:** gaps 1–6 are a single coherent **CLE/CME compliance bundle** that no WordPress plugin ships and that maps 1:1 to what accreditors mandate. Built together, they make MediaShield the only WP player that can back accredited continuing-education credit — exactly the BeyondTheCLE thesis. This is a far stronger position than chasing OTT parity (gaps 8–10).
